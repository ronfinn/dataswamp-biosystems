# Commercial licensing

## The short version

The **software** is MIT — commercial use included, no permission needed, no
separate licence.

The **official generated benchmark datasets and bundles distributed by the
project** (v0.1.0 onward) are CC BY-NC 4.0. That licence grants noncommercial use
only. **Commercial use of that material requires separate permission from the
project owner.**

See [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) and [`DATA-LICENSE.md`](DATA-LICENSE.md).

## What counts as commercial use

CC BY-NC 4.0 defines a NonCommercial use as one not primarily intended for or
directed toward commercial advantage or monetary compensation. Examples of uses
of the official benchmark material that need separate permission:

- **commercial AI, model or agent evaluation** — benchmarking a product,
  service or model as part of commercial development, marketing or sales;
- redistributing the material inside a paid product, dataset or service;
- internal use at a for-profit organisation in support of a commercial offering;
- consulting or training delivered for a fee using the material.

Noncommercial personal, academic, nonprofit and educational research — including
noncommercial academic AI/model/agent evaluation — is already permitted under
CC BY-NC 4.0 and needs no further permission.

## If you are unsure

Two things usually resolve it without a licence conversation:

1. **Use the software, not the distributed data.** Generating your own estate
   from the MIT-licensed generators puts you outside the scope of the data
   licence entirely.
2. **Ask.** Open an issue on the repository describing the intended use. The
   project owner grants commercial permissions individually; terms are not
   published in advance.

This document describes the project's licensing position. It is not legal advice.
